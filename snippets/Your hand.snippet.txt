Your hand

When I'm in a state of anxiety
A nervous, almost shaking worry,
I know this fog will quickly fade
When our two hands are made. 
Intertwined fingers, a comforting hold. 
Like those nostalgic tales told. 
For no matter where we are
I know your hand won't be far.

Reason/Description:
Earlier in the day, I read a poem that my friend had shared with me. It was from a poet I didn't know. It was interesting to me. It followed more standard metres than I'd been reading lately. It reminded me of when I first started writing and how I liked to challenge myself by writing within strict metres. In the evening, a little before bed, I was reading a book (Termush by Jeff Vandermeer) and there was a small line about how the protagonist placed their hand on their partner for reassurance and it gave me a blindingly clear thought about how, when I'm worried or stressed, I hold my partners hand and I often get this flooding feeling of all my worries escaping from me. So I figured I'd try and write something about that with metres for a change. This is what I came up with in the 30 minutes before bed.
