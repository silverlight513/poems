Working title: Life in the city (rework the title)

1.
Imagine, for a moment, 
you're standing on the side 
of an empty asphalt road.

2.
There's no cars, no people.
Just towering office blocks
following one long road.

3.
So you walk, along this empty street.
Inspecting the buildings you pass.
They all look the same. // This doesn't vibe but has the right content

4.
To your left, inside the building, atop the front desk. 
There's a small brass nameplate.
Ready for guests to address an empty chair.

5.
There's no one inside. 
There's no one to greet.
Just an empty seat.

6.
You try to get in, 
pulling and pushing.
But the door doesn't budge.

7.
So you continue down the street.
walking by more office blocks.
You pass ten and ten more.

8.
Inside each and every one,
sits an empty chair
and a new name, on their small brass nameplate.

9.
The road keeps going,
the offices continue towering.
And you're here, on the road.

10.
You're alone
