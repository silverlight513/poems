Willful kindness

When I'm tired, and empty of will
I shirk myself to selfish thoughts.
Demanding what isn't mine.
But on the days I try,
The days I try to be kind,
More compassionate and loving
I'm a happier person
And those around me
Open their hearts a little more.
So now, even when i'm tired,
I try a little harder to be kind.

Reason/Description:
One day, while lying in bed, trying to fall asleep I realised I had been tired all day and a little grouchy. More grouchy than I would have normally been. This made me wonder, if I was purposefully happier would I see improvements in my life, my relationships, everything? The next day I tested my theory and realised it was working. I didn't know if I could make this change permenant but I could try. That night, I recapped my thoughts and wrote out this quick poem to summize my experience so far.
