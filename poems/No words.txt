No words

I don't know what's left. 
I've got no words to write.
Is this my new self? 
One without a fight?

My feelings grow shallower
becoming almost non-existant.
Other than this pain
making my heart so distant.

Life has turned upside down,
from a happy streak
to an unknown track.
Everything just looks so bleak.

There's nowhere to turn,
so I grow more withdrawn.
Hoping for new life
with every new dawn.
