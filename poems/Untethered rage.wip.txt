Working title: Untethered rage

1.
I don't miss the rage
that outgrew
my mental cage. 

2.
When it drew itself
outside the footnotes
of my diary page.

3.
I hide it on a shelf.
Hoping it'd die and rot
Removed from myself.

4.
But it festered
And it boiled 
Right off its stage. 

5.
People grew bothered
And love became pain. 
The anger untethered. 

6.

Reason/Description:
I was listening to some Haley Williams albums which often talks about upset and internal rage. This got me reminiscing about my past, when my unchecked ADHD would cause me to rage, strongly, without mercy. I've since gone to therapy and can handle myself in a way that stops these events happening, for the sake of all those around me.
